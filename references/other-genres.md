# Essays and academic personal statements

Apply common clarity/fidelity checks plus P43 or P44. Do not impose IMRaD.

## Essays and scientific commentary

Identify thesis, reader, evidence, and progression. Orient before technical detail
and use concrete examples. Use vivid accurate language and coherent metaphors
only where they clarify; do not extend metaphors beyond supported relationships.
A return to an opening theme is optional. Avoid irrelevant technical detours,
unsupported certainty, and confusion between evidence and opinion.

## Research-oriented personal statements

Select experiences demonstrating motivation, readiness, trajectory, and specific
fit. Do not restate the CV or replace evidence with adjectives. Use only supplied
stories: never invent achievements, adversity, identity, conversations, or program
facts. With thin material, provide a bounded revision and list missing examples
separately. Explain relevant setbacks and learning honestly, without compelling
unrelated personal disclosures. Connect opening and conclusion where useful and
follow the actual application prompt, length, and known rules.
