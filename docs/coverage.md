## Additional modules

Review articles, reviewer responses, public communication, grants, and recommendations
extend genre routing while reusing the same IDs and safeguards. They do not change
the 44-principle catalogue or imply technical peer review. See E33–E34 and E37,
E46–E48 for relevant extension scenarios.
